# Contributing to FullDex

Thank you for helping improve FullDex.

FullDex welcomes bug fixes, new features, documentation improvements, translations and platform compatibility work.

## Submitting contributions

Please open an issue or pull request explaining your proposed change.

Contributions should:

- Be original work or material you are authorised to submit.
- Follow the applicable licences of the files being modified.
- Preserve existing copyright, licence and attribution notices.
- Clearly identify any third-party code and its source and licence.
- Explain significant changes and include relevant testing information.
- Avoid submitting copyrighted game ROMs or game assets without appropriate permission.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the rights necessary to provide it under the applicable project licence.

## Copyright and credit

Contributors retain copyright in their own contributions unless otherwise agreed.

Contributions are recorded through Git commits and pull requests.

Do not remove or misrepresent existing authorship and copyright notices.

## Community builds

FullDex is open source, and redistribution is permitted in accordance with the applicable licences.

If you distribute a modified build, please:

- Identify it clearly as an unofficial version.
- Explain your modifications.
- Preserve required legal notices and source-code access.
- Link to the original FullDex project as a courtesy.
- Avoid implying endorsement by the FullDex maintainers.
